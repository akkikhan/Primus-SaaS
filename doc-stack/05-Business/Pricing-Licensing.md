# 🏷️ Pricing & Licensing Framework

**Model:** Annual Subscription per Organization.  
**Philosophy:** Simple, transparent, no "per-user" penalties.

---

## 1. 💎 Tiers

| Feature | **Starter** | **Pro (Recommended)** | **Enterprise** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Modules** | Identity, Logging | All Modules | All Modules + Beta Access |
| **Projects** | 1 App | Unlimited Apps | Unlimited Apps |
| **Support** | Community | Email (24h SLA) | Dedicated Slack Channel |
| **Source Code** | Compiled (NuGet) | Compiled (NuGet) | **Full Source Access** |
| **Price** | **Free** | **$499 / mo** | **Contact Sales** |

---

## 2. 📜 Licensing Terms

*   **Perpetual Use:** If you cancel, you can keep using the version you have installed. You just lose access to updates.
*   **Royalty Free:** You can ship as many apps as you want. We don't charge per end-user.
*   **No Resale:** You cannot wrap Primus SDKs and sell them as a competing SDK product.

---

## 3. 💳 Billing FAQ

*   **Q: Do you charge for Dev/Staging environments?**
    *   A: No. Only production usage counts towards "Projects" in the Starter tier. Pro is unlimited.
*   **Q: Can I upgrade later?**
    *   A: Yes, instantly.
