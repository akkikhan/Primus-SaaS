*   **Shift to Microservices:** Favors modular SDKs over monolithic boilerplates.
*   **Data Sovereignty:** Companies want to own their data/runtime (Anti-SaaS-lock-in).
*   **DevEx:** Developer Experience is the new buying criteria. Primus excels here.
