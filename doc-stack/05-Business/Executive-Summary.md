# 👔 Executive Summary

**Product:** Primus SaaS Platform  
**Mission:** Accelerate SaaS delivery by 10x through modular, client-owned SDKs.

---

## 1. 🌍 The Problem

Building a modern SaaS application requires solving the same problems over and over:
*   **Identity:** Auth0/AzureAD integration, token validation, security.
*   **Observability:** Logging, PII masking, tracing.
*   **Communication:** Email/SMS templates, provider management.

Development teams spend **30-40% of their time** building this "plumbing" instead of core business value.

---

## 2. 💡 The Solution: Primus SaaS

Primus provides a suite of **production-grade, drop-in modules** for these common capabilities.
*   **Not a SaaS Wrapper:** We don't host your data.
*   **Not a Framework:** We don't force a specific architecture.
*   **Just Libraries:** We ship NuGet/npm packages you own and control.

---

## 3. 📈 Key Benefits

1.  **Speed:** Launch features in days, not months.
2.  **Security:** Enterprise-grade security (PII masking, strict auth) out of the box.
3.  **Control:** Zero vendor lock-in on runtime. You host it, you own it.
4.  **Consistency:** Every microservice logs, authenticates, and notifies the exact same way.

---

## 4. 🎯 Strategic Goals

*   **Q1:** Release Core Modules (Identity, Logging, Notifications).
*   **Q2:** Onboard 5 Pilot Teams.
*   **Q3:** Expand to Billing & Tenancy modules.
*   **Q4:** Public GA.
