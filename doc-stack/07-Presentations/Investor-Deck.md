## Slide 2: The Problem

### "Why is building SaaS so slow?"

*   **Repetitive Work:** Every team rebuilds Auth, Logging, and Notifications.
*   **Security Risks:** Junior devs implementing JWT validation often get it wrong.
*   **Compliance Nightmares:** GDPR/PII handling is an afterthought.
*   **Cost:** 30% of engineering budget goes to "plumbing", not features.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution

### Primus SaaS SDKs

A suite of modular, production-grade libraries that solve the hard parts.

*   **Identity:** Multi-provider (Azure AD + Auth0) out of the box.
*   **Logging:** Automatic PII redaction and correlation.
*   **Notifications:** Templated email/SMS engine.

**Key Differentiator:** "Client-Owned". We don't host your data. You do.
