## 🎨 COLOR GRADING GUIDE

### OLD Aesthetic (Discard):
- Muted tones
- Heavy desaturation
- Moody shadows
- Vintage/faded look

### NEW Aesthetic (Use This):
- **Vibrant but natural** colors
- **Bright** exposure (not dark/moody)
- **Warm** tones (but not orange)
- **Crisp** clarity (not overly soft)
- **Saturation:** 110-120% (slightly boosted)
- **Contrast:** Medium-high (not flat)

### Reference Style:
- Think: **Emma Chamberlain** (bright, vibrant, joyful)
- NOT: **Sad girl autumn** (moody, muted, melancholic)
