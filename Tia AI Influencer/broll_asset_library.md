## 🎨 Creative Reuse Strategy

### The "Remix" Technique
**Problem:** Limited B-roll assets
**Solution:** Reuse the same clips with different treatments

**Example:**
- `tia_video1_broll_street.png` can be used in:
  - Video #1 (travel intro)
  - Video #3 (getting lost)
  - Video #4 (solo travel advice)
  - Video #5 (day in the life)

**How to Make It Look Different:**
1. **Zoom:** Crop differently each time
2. **Color Grade:** Warmer for cozy, cooler for "lost"
3. **Speed:** Slow-mo for aesthetic, normal for storytelling
4. **Overlay:** Add text, stickers, or filters

### Asset Rotation Schedule
**Week 1 (Videos 1-5):** Use all 9 assets freely
**Week 2 (Videos 6-10):** Rotate + add new B-roll (quota resets)
