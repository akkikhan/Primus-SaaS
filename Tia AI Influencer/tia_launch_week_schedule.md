# Tia - Launch Week Schedule (Day-by-Day)

**Objective:** A disciplined 7-day sprint to launch the channel and trigger the algorithm.

## Day 1: The Foundation (Monday)
*   [ ] **Create Accounts:** Gmail, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram (secure the handle).
*   [ ] **Upload Assets:** Set PFP and Banner on all platforms.
*   [ ] **Bio Setup:** Copy/Paste bios from `tia_social_profiles.md`.
*   [ ] **Voice Test:** Generate 3 versions of the "Hello World" script using `tia_voice_settings.md` and pick the best one.

## Day 2: The Factory (Tuesday)
*   [ ] **Generate Visuals:** Create the 5 clips for Video #1 (using `tia_video_1_production_pack.md`).
*   [ ] **Edit Video #1:** Assemble in CapCut. Add captions and music.
*   [ ] **Thumbnail:** Finalize the thumbnail text overlay.
*   [ ] **Pre-Generate:** Generate 3 extra "B-Roll" clips for future use (Tia walking, Tia eating).

## Day 3: LAUNCH DAY 🚀 (Wednesday)
*   [ ] **10:00 AM:** Upload Video #1 to YouTube Shorts & TikTok.
*   [ ] **10:05 AM:** Post the "Spark Comments" (from `tia_video_1_engagement.md`) using alt accounts.
*   [ ] **10:10 AM:** Post to Instagram Stories: "Finally did it! First vlog is up! 🙈 link in bio."
*   [ ] **12:00 PM:** Reply to any real comments immediately.

## Day 4: The Community (Thursday)
*   [ ] **YouTube Community Post:** "I can't believe I actually posted that! 😅 Where should I go next? A) Italy 🇮🇹 B) Spain 🇪🇸"
*   [ ] **Instagram Post:** Photo of Tia with coffee. Caption: "Morning views. ☕ Did you see the vlog yet?"
*   [ ] **Scripting:** Write script for Video #2 ("My Morning Routine in Paris").

## Day 5: Momentum (Friday)
*   [ ] **Production:** Generate and Edit Video #2.
*   [ ] **Upload Video #2:** Post to Shorts/TikTok at 5:00 PM (Peak time).
*   [ ] **Engagement:** Spend 20 mins scrolling related hashtags (#solotravel, #paris) and commenting as Tia.

## Day 6: Cross-Pollination (Saturday)
*   [ ] **TikTok Exclusive:** Post a "Behind the Scenes" or "Blooper" (e.g., "AI glitch" joke or just a funny outtake).
*   [ ] **Pinterest:** Pin the Thumbnail image to a "Travel Aesthetic" board (links back to YouTube).

## Day 7: Review & Rest (Sunday)
*   [ ] **Analytics Check:**
    *   Did Video 1 or 2 perform better?
    *   Where did viewers drop off? (Retention graph).
*   [ ] **Rest:** Do not post. Let the algorithm breathe.
*   [ ] **Plan:** Outline Week 2 based on data.
