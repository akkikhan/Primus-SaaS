## Instagram Stories Strategy

### Daily Stories (3-5 per day):
1. **Morning:** "Good morning from Paris ☕" (Coffee shot)
2. **Midday:** Behind-the-scenes of content creation
3. **Evening:** Poll or Question sticker ("What should I film tomorrow?")
4. **Night:** "Goodnight" story with sunset/cozy shot

### Weekly Stories:
- **Monday:** "Week ahead" preview
- **Wednesday:** "Hump day" relatable meme
- **Friday:** "Weekend plans" poll
- **Sunday:** "Week recap" highlights

---

## Engagement Strategy

### Comment Response Rules:
1. Reply to EVERY comment in the first hour
2. Reply to ALL comments within 24 hours
3. Use the commenter's name when possible
4. Ask a follow-up question to encourage more replies

### Community Building:
- Like 50 comments per day on similar creators' posts
- Follow 20-30 accounts in your niche per day
- Unfollow accounts that don't follow back after 3 days
- DM 5 micro-influencers per week for collaboration

## Notes:
- **Consistency > Perfection:** Post even if the video isn't "perfect"
- **Engage First, Post Second:** Spend 30 mins engaging with others before posting
- **Save Everything:** Keep a "Content Ideas" note on your phone
- **Batch Create:** Film 3-5 videos in one day, post throughout the week
- **Rest Days:** It's okay to skip a day if you're burnt out (but tell your audience why)
