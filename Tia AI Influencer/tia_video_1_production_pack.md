# Tia - Video #1: "Hello World" (Production Pack)

**Concept:** A candid, slightly nervous, but warm introduction. It should feel like a FaceTime call or a quick vlog update, not a polished TV commercial.
**Goal:** Establish the "Girl Next Door" vibe and the "Travel" niche.

---

## 1. The Script (Audio)
**Voice Direction:** Young American female, soft, slightly raspy.
**Crucial:** Use **ElevenLabs**. Set Stability to ~35% for more emotion.
**Text to Generate:**
> "Okay... is this thing on? *[nervous laugh]*
>
> Hi everyone! I’m Tia.
>
> I decided to finally stop overthinking it and just... start. I’m currently traveling through Europe, trying to find the best coffee, and honestly? Just trying not to get lost every five minutes.
>
> I’ll be sharing my favorite spots, some travel tips, and probably a lot of messy moments too. So... yeah! Stay cozy, and I'll catch you at the next stop."

## 3. Editing Instructions (CapCut / Premiere)
1.  **Music:** Search for "Lo-fi Chill Hop" or "Acoustic Guitar Travel." Keep volume at -20dB (background only).
2.  **Captions:** Use a "Typewriter" or "Karaoke" style animation. Font: **Montserrat** or **Courier New** (clean but classic). Color: White with a thin black outline.
3.  **Effects:** Add a very subtle "Film Grain" overlay (5% opacity) to the whole video to glue the AI clips together.
